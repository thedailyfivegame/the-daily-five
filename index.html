How to Play

Rules

1
You have 6 attempts to guess the secret 5-letter word.
2
Type any valid word and press Enter to submit your guess.
3
After each guess, tile colours show how close you are to the answer.
4
One puzzle per day — a new word drops at midnight.
5
Sign in to appear on the leaderboard and earn coins.

Tile Colours

W
Green — right letter, right position
A
Yellow — letter is in the word, wrong spot
T
Grey — letter is not in the word
Tip: Start with words that use many common letters — like CRANE, AUDIO, or STARE.

Rules

1
You are shown 5 scrambled letter tiles — unscramble them to find the word.
2
Tap a tile to place it in the next answer slot. The timer starts on your first tap.
3
Tap a filled slot to remove letters from that position onwards.
4
Once all 5 slots are filled, your answer submits automatically.
5
Your final time is your score — the lower the better!
