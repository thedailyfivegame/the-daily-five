Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

The Daily Five ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website thedailyfive.net and the mobile application The Daily Five (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Game data stored locally: Your game progress, statistics (win streak, guess distribution), and preferences (dark mode, hard mode, etc.) are stored only on your device using browser localStorage or mobile device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Account information (optional): You may optionally sign in using your Google account. When you do, we receive from Google:

  • Your display name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo URL
  • A unique Google user ID

This information is stored securely in Google Firebase and is used to personalise your experience and link your game history across devices. Creating an account is entirely optional.

Leaderboard name: If you choose to appear on the daily leaderboard, you provide a display name. This name and your daily score (number of guesses) are stored in our database and are publicly visible to other players.

Email address (optional): You may optionally provide your email address to receive daily game reminders. Your email is stored in our database and used only to send you notifications about The Daily Five. We will never share it with third parties or use it for marketing beyond game-related communications.

Push notification token (mobile app): If you grant notification permission on the Android app, an Expo push token is stored in our database. This token is used solely to deliver daily word reminders to your device. It is not linked to your identity unless you are also signed in.

Automatically collected information: When you use the Service, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Device type, operating system, and browser type
  • IP address and general geographic region (country/city level)
  • Pages visited and interactions within the Service
  • Date and time of access

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Display your name and score on the public leaderboard (only if you opt in)
  • Send daily word reminders (only if you opt in via email or push notifications)
  • Personalise your experience if you sign in with Google
  • Understand how users interact with the game via analytics
  • Serve and measure advertising
  • Detect and prevent technical issues or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve the Service. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the game to function (e.g. storing today's game state, sign-in session).
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand how visitors use the site (Google Analytics).
  • Advertising cookies: Used by our advertising partners to serve relevant ads.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect game functionality and sign-in.

4. Advertising

The Daily Five is free to play and is supported by advertising.

Web: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on thedailyfive.net. Google may use cookies and device identifiers to show you personalised ads based on your interests. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Mobile app: The Daily Five Android app may display advertisements via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the Service:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, Firebase Authentication) — stores account data, leaderboard entries, push tokens, and email subscriptions. Data is stored in Google's secure cloud infrastructure. See Firebase Privacy.
  • Google Analytics — anonymised usage analytics.
  • Google AdSense / AdMob — advertising.
  • Expo (by Expo.io) — delivers push notifications to the Android app.
  • Vercel — website hosting.

Each of these providers has their own privacy policy and data practices.

6. Google Sign-In

When you use "Continue with Google", you are redirected to Google's sign-in page. We only receive the information Google provides after you authenticate (name, email, profile picture, user ID). We do not receive or store your Google password. You can revoke our access to your Google account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers: Third parties who help us operate the Service (listed in Section 5) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Other players: Your leaderboard display name and daily score are visible to other users if you choose to submit a score.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

8. Children's Privacy

The Daily Five is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the Service:

  • Leaderboard entries: Scores are associated with a specific day and may be retained indefinitely for historical records.
  • Account data: If you signed in with Google, your account data is retained until you request deletion.
  • Push tokens: Retained until you uninstall the app or revoke notification permissions.
  • Email subscriptions: Retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Local game data: Stored only on your device; clear it anytime by clearing browser localStorage or uninstalling the app.

To request deletion of any data we hold about you, email us at thedailyfive.game@gmail.com.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us directly.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, you also have rights under GDPR including the right to object to processing and the right to data portability.

If you are a California resident, you have rights under CCPA including the right to know what personal data is collected and the right to opt out of sale (we do not sell personal data).

11. Security

We use industry-standard measures including Google Firebase's security infrastructure to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Third-Party Links

The Service may contain links to third-party websites (e.g. app stores). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
