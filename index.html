Frequently asked questions

What is The Daily Five?
The Daily Five is a free daily word puzzle game where you guess a five-letter word in up to six tries. A new word is revealed every day at midnight, and everyone in the world plays the same word on the same day.
How is The Daily Five different from Wordle?
The Daily Five is a Wordle alternative with extra polish: a warm newspaper aesthetic, solve timer, word definition reveal after each game, streak milestones, difficulty badges, dark mode, hard mode, color-blind mode, and it installs as a PWA — all without signing in or creating an account.
Is The Daily Five free to play?
Completely free — no subscription, no sign-up, no lives to buy. The game itself will always be free.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Yes. The Daily Five runs in any modern browser on iOS and Android. You can also add it to your home screen as a progressive web app (PWA) for a native-app experience, complete with offline support.
How often does the word change?
One new word every day at midnight. Everyone gets the same word on the same day, so you can compare emoji grids with friends without spoilers.
What do the colored tiles mean?
Green means the letter is in the correct position. Amber (yellow) means the letter is in the word but in the wrong spot. Gray means the letter is not in the word at all.
What is Hard Mode?
In Hard Mode, any letter hint you reveal must be used in all subsequent guesses. It prevents cheap guessing strategies and makes the game significantly more challenging. Enable it in Settings before you start guessing.
Can I play offline?
Yes — after your first visit, The Daily Five installs as a PWA and works fully offline. Just add it to your home screen for the best experience.
Will my stats and streak reset?
Your stats and streak are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data or switching to a different device will reset them. Adding the game to your home screen (as a PWA) helps preserve your data for longer.
What are the streak milestones?
The game celebrates milestone streaks with special toast messages: 7 days in a row earns 🔥 "on fire", 30 days earns 🏆 "legendary", and 100 days earns 💎 "unstoppable". Your streak also appears in your share text so friends can see it.
