Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who runs The Daily Five

The Daily Five is an independent puzzle site run by Sunny. This policy covers thedailyfive.net and its Home Screen web app. Contact thedailyfive.game@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

What we use and why

Local gameplay. Your browser or Home Screen app saves game progress, play dates, streaks, coins and preferences on your device. The app can have its own storage separate from Safari. Clearing browser or app data may remove that local history.

Accounts. Accounts are optional. Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in details and assigns an account identifier. For Google sign-in, the site can receive your email address, name and profile photo from Google. We use account and game information to sign you in, show your account and support limited recovery of the current Word result and saved coins. Older history and streaks can remain on the device where you played.

Public rankings. Publishing a score is optional. If you publish, the score record includes your nickname, result, puzzle day, submission time and identifiers used to associate the score with your account or guest profile. The public rankings display your nickname and results. The underlying score records, including their identifiers, are publicly readable. Choose a nickname without personal details. Clearing local data or signing out does not remove a score already published.

Support and reminders. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you send so we can respond. If you previously opted into a reminder, we may hold the email address or notification token you supplied. Contact us to ask for it to be removed. Turning off notification permissions on your device stops that device receiving web notifications.

Hosting, storage and external services

We use Vercel to host the site and Google Firebase for sign-in and game data. Hosting and service providers can receive technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver and protect the service. Google Fonts serves some of the site's fonts. These services may process information in other countries.

The site uses browser storage for gameplay and sign-in. Google Analytics and advertising tags have been paused. If you follow the optional support link, Ko-fi and its payment providers handle the payment under their own terms and privacy policies. The Daily Five website does not collect your payment card details. Vercel Privacy Policy · Google Privacy Policy · Ko-fi Privacy Policy

Retention and privacy requests

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you, your browser or your device removes it. Published scores can remain visible after their ranking period ends. We retain account, ranking and support records as needed to provide the relevant feature, resolve disputes, protect the service or meet legal obligations.

To request access, correction, deletion, removal of a public score or removal from reminders, email thedailyfive.game@gmail.com. For a score request, include the nickname, game and approximate puzzle date. We may ask for enough information to verify that the record is yours. Never send your password. Depending on local law, you may have additional rights and may be able to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The site is for a general audience. Children who are under the age at which they can consent under applicable law should avoid creating an account, publishing scores or sending personal details unless a parent or guardian has authorised it as local law permits. A parent or guardian can contact us if they believe a child supplied personal information so we can review it and take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We publish changes on this page and update the date above. If a change requires additional notice or permission, we will provide it before applying the change.
