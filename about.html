By Sunny  ·  thedailyfivegame@gmail.com  ·  Est. 2024

The Daily Five started with a simple frustration: I wanted a word game that went beyond one puzzle a day. Wordle is great — but what if you could warm up with a scramble, test your trivia, and then take on the main word challenge, all in one place?

So I built it. Every game resets at midnight. Every puzzle is the same for every player around the world. No algorithms, no endless scrolling, no ads interrupting your flow — just five minutes of good mental exercise before you get on with your day.

"A five-minute mental workout. New games every day. No sign-up needed."

Why free?

Word games should be for everyone. There's no paywall, no premium tier, no "you've used your free plays for today." You can play every day, share your results, compete on the leaderboard, and earn coins — all without spending a thing.

The site runs on a shoestring. Hosting, the domain, the puzzle database — it all costs real money. If you enjoy the games, the best way to keep The Daily Five alive and growing is to tell a friend, share your results, or buy us a coffee.

The leaderboard

Sign in with your Google account and your name appears on the global leaderboard. Your wins across all three games count toward your monthly score. Top players get the gold, silver, and bronze medals. Rankings reset each month so there's always a fresh race.
