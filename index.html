Yes — completely free to play, forever. There are small banner ads on the homepage to keep the lights on, but never mid-game ads. The hints system lets you optionally watch an ad for coins.

No account needed to play. Google Sign-In is optional and only required if you want to sync your streak across devices or appear on the leaderboard with your name.

You earn coins by winning the daily puzzle (10 coins base + bonus for fewer guesses and streaks). Spend coins on hints: reveal a letter (5🪙), check if a letter is in the word (3🪙), or get an extra guess (8🪙). Watch a short ad to earn 5 bonus coins.

Yes — one new word at midnight every day. You get six guesses to find it. Words are common English words; we avoid proper nouns, plurals of proper nouns, and overly obscure terms.

Hard Mode requires you to use all revealed hints in every subsequent guess. If you discover the letter E is correct in position 3, your next guess must have E in position 3. It's significantly more challenging.
