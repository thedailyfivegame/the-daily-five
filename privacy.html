Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2025  ·  Effective immediately

The Daily Five ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website thedailyfive.net and the mobile application The Daily Five (collectively, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: The Daily Five does not require account registration and does not ask you to submit personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details to play the game.

Automatically collected information: When you use the Service, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Device type, operating system, and browser type
  • IP address and general geographic region (country/city level)
  • Pages visited and interactions within the Service
  • Date and time of access

Game data stored locally: Your game progress, statistics (win streak, guess distribution), and preferences (dark mode, hard mode, etc.) are stored only on your device using browser localStorage or mobile device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and improve the Service. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the game to function (e.g. storing today's game state).
  • Analytics cookies: Used to understand how visitors use the site (e.g. Google Analytics, if enabled).
  • Advertising cookies: Used by our advertising partners (see Section 3) to serve relevant ads.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect game functionality.

3. Advertising

The Daily Five is free to play and is supported by advertising.

Web: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on thedailyfive.net. Google may use cookies and device identifiers to show you personalised ads based on your interests. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Mobile app: The Daily Five mobile app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

4. In-App Purchases

The mobile app offers an optional one-time purchase ("Remove Ads") processed through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store your payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple or Google under their respective terms and privacy policies.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Understand how users interact with the game
  • Serve and measure advertising
  • Detect and prevent technical issues or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers: Third parties who help us operate the Service (e.g. hosting, analytics, advertising) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.

7. Children's Privacy

The Daily Five is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal account data, there is no user data to delete on our end. Game data stored on your device can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or uninstalling the mobile app.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we collect minimal data, most requests can be fulfilled by clearing your browser data or contacting us directly.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, you also have rights under GDPR including the right to object to processing and the right to data portability.

10. Security
