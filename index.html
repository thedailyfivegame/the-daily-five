Frequently asked questions

What is The Daily Five?
The Daily Five is a free daily word puzzle game where you guess a five-letter word in up to six tries. A new word is revealed every day at midnight, and everyone in the world plays the same word on the same day.
What makes The Daily Five stand out?
The Daily Five is a free daily word game with extra polish: a warm newspaper aesthetic, solve timer, word definition reveal after each game, streak milestones, difficulty badges, dark mode, hard mode, color-blind mode, a daily leaderboard, and it installs as a PWA — all without signing in.
Is The Daily Five free to play?
Completely free — no subscription, no sign-up, and no lives to buy. The game itself will always be free.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Yes — it works in any browser on iPhone, Android, or desktop. You can also install it to your home screen as a PWA for an app-like experience with offline support.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Once you've loaded the game once, it works offline thanks to the service worker cache.
Will my stats and streak reset?
Stats are saved in your browser's local storage. They persist as long as you don't clear your browser data. They are not tied to an account, so clearing cache will reset them.
What are the streak milestones?
Streaks of 3, 7, 14, 30, and 100 days earn special badges shown in your stats. Keep coming back every day to climb the milestones.
